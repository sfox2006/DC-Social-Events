About

What this is

A curated listing of upcoming social events in Washington, DC, including in-person, hybrid, and online — music, comedy, food, nightlife, markets, outdoors, and more. It is updated as new events are announced.

Window
The list shows upcoming events from today through about the next year, in Eastern Time. The calendar pages about 3 months ahead and marks those days.
Sources
Public venue and organizer calendars, newsletters, and social posts. Details are checked against the source pages.
Times

Maintenance
Updated daily. A pipeline publishes the event list.
